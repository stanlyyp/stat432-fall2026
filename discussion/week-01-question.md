### Connecting Fragmented Concepts: How to Systematically Rebuild Prerequisites with AI?

Using AI assistants allows me to break down complex tasks and master specific mathematical mechanics problem by problem. However, this problem-centric approach leaves me with **fragmented knowledge**. I can solve individual questions, but I struggle to connect isolated concepts into a cohesive framework for statistical learning. I often feel like I am using tools without seeing the underlying architecture.

**Systematic AI-Assisted Strategy:** How can we leverage AI beyond solving isolated homework problems to systematically build an integrated knowledge graph, without getting overwhelmed by re-studying three full prerequisite courses from scratch?
